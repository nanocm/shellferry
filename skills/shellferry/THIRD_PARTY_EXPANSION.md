## Inherited code and distribution scope

GPL, LGPL, EPL, and MPL portions retain their original terms, including the exclusions identified in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). cargo-dist template versions are separately pinned. shlib, godownloader, Deno, Starship, rustup, and supports-hyperlinks ancestry is retained wherever identified by the installer.

OpenTofu's installer has an Apache-2.0 declaration even though its repository root license is MPL-2.0; both texts are included. Fly's hosted installer identifies Deno authors and MIT, and is pinned by content hash rather than a claimed source commit.

These notices cover the source/skill distribution. Downloaded application binaries, Python wheels, plugins, runtimes, and source archives are not shipped in the skill. Review their separate redistribution terms before publishing a generated bundle.
